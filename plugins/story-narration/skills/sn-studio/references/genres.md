# Genre cards for narrated story channels

The genre table is an **sn synthesis for Vietnamese story channels** (no source covers them directly);
the payoff types and opponent tiers below are from short-drama. Each card: core emotion the viewer comes for, the promise of the premise (what "fun & games" must
deliver), a typical engine, and pitfalls. Sources: short-drama `genre-guide`, oh-story genre→emotion
table, jtydhr88 `sw-genre-anatomy`. Genres can be stacked (e.g. family drama + revenge); pick one
**primary** emotion — it decides the payoff types.

| Genre (vi label) | Core emotion | Premise promise | Engine | Pitfalls |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gia đình / tình thân | ấm, xót, day dứt | Hidden sacrifice or old wound finally understood | Secret from the past resurfaces; generations misunderstand each other | Melodrama without cause; villain parent with no reason |
| Báo thù / vả mặt (revenge, face-slap) | hả hê (release) | The humiliated rises and turns the tables | Humiliation → hidden preparation → public reversal, escalating tiers of opponents | Humiliation too mild → payoff cheap; hero acts above the law |
| Trọng sinh / xuyên không | thỏa mãn, tò mò | Second chance: knowledge of the future changes fate | Butterfly effect; old enemies don't know what she knows | Hero omniscient → no tension; keep one thing she didn't foresee |
| Tình cảm / ngược | xao xuyến, đau | A bond we believe in, then tested | Bond-laying → tearing → lingering ache | Misunderstanding solvable by one conversation |
| Huyền bí / kinh dị dân gian | rờn rợn, tò mò | A rule of the unseen world, broken | Rule established → violated → escalating signs → truth of the curse | Explaining the ghost fully kills the dread |
| Trinh thám / bí ẩn | tò mò, "à ra thế" | A fair-play puzzle | Clue → deduction → reversal loop; red herrings debunked | Unplanted solution; clue shown only visually |
| Dã sử / cổ trang | hùng tráng, bi | An individual against the tide of history | Loyalty vs. survival dilemmas; court intrigue rounds | Info-dumping history; anachronistic speech |
| Cổ tích / ngụ ngôn hiện đại | ấm, suy ngẫm | Kindness or cunning rewarded | Trials of three; a magical rule with a cost | Narrator preaching the moral |
| Chữa lành / đời thường | bình yên, chạm | Small change in a stuck life | Encounter → small rituals → a choice to open up | No turn = no story; keep a real want and obstacle |
| Nghị lực / đổi đời | cảm phục | From nothing to something through effort | Setback ladder, mentor, public proof | Rags-to-riches by luck; sermonising |

## Satisfaction payoff types (short-drama 爽点 matrix, generalised)

Every payoff = **pressure → release**; the deeper the pressure, the stronger the release.

1. **Identity reveal** — the underestimated shows who they really are. Needs a humiliation first, a
   ritual reveal, and witnesses' reactions (reactions amplify the payoff — always narrate them).
2. **Turning the tables** — the villain's own weapon backfires (their words quoted back, evidence at the
   last second, a trap within a trap).
3. **Comeback** — rock bottom → turning point → climb.
4. **Sweetness / tenderness** — protection, recognition, reunion, confession.
5. **Truth revealed** — the mystery solved; the misunderstood vindicated.

Intensity scale ★★ (1–3 people know) → ★★★★★ (everyone, final reveal). Spend the ★★★★★ once, in the
showdown. Space payoffs so no 10-minute stretch lacks one.

## Opponent tiers (short-drama villain system)

Minor (early fodder, beaten by ~20%) → middle (main rival of Act 2, beaten around 60–75%) → major (final
opponent) → optional hidden opponent (the "it's not over" twist, must be planted). Make each believable
(a want the audience understands) and hateable (a concrete cruelty shown, not reported). Defeat in
ascending order; the major opponent's defeat should express the theme.
